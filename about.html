History & Profile

Our Rich History

The descriptive name Bulua arises from ancestral terminology highlighting the clear, bubbling freshwater springs that once ran through this coastal zone. As it evolved, Bulua transformed cleanly from a coastal farming settlement into a lively commercial center. Crucially, local artisan families preserved the meticulous techniques of hand-shaping earth clay into functional jars, establishing Bulua as the iconic pottery destination of Cagayan de Oro.
