Welcome to Barangay Bulua

The Dynamic Western Gateway of Cagayan de Oro City.

Barangay Bulua serves as a critical commercial transit and craft hub within Northern Mindanao. Proudly preserving traditional artistry in clay pottery while advancing regional transportation networks, our community links deep cultural heritage with fast economic growth.
