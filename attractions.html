Notable Places & Landmarks

Westbound Public Terminal

The core regional transportation complex connecting western CDO to neighboring western cities and municipalities.

San Roque Parish Church

The prominent spiritual sanctuary anchoring the devotion and rich community celebrations of Bulua families.

Culture & Famous Local Products

Bulua Traditional Clay Pottery Stalls

Celebrated handcrafted jars, custom clay frames, and architectural plant containers manufactured right by resident artisans.

The Annual Feast of San Roque

Held every August, this grand festival brings sports games, street programs, and cultural craft markets directly to our avenues.

Educational Institutions

Bulua National High School

A modern high school tracking academic advancement and technical-vocational pathways for neighborhood students.

Bulua Central Elementary School

The essential foundational center providing robust child training and structural education steps within our urban layout.

Barangay Bulua

A community information website celebrating service, heritage, and bayanihan.
